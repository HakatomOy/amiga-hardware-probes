# A500 BLTBDAT hold measurements, 2026-10-17

This note records the held-out kind48 measurements for BLTBDAT hold behavior.
The broader blitter follow-up was motivated by [Toni Wilen's Copper-start
observation in EAB post 1426048](https://eab.abime.net/showpost.php?p=1426048)
and [register-handoff discussion in post
1422393](https://eab.abime.net/showpost.php?p=1422393). The retained-B rules
below were established independently by A500 measurements. The physical run
used a One ROM board with live ROM replacement and its result collector. These
are observations from one A500, not a claim about every Agnus revision.

## Held-out line-shifter matrix

The matrix has 1,152 rows: raw B words `$0001,$8000,$a55a,$1248`, BSHIFT
`0,1,7,15`, line-prime lengths one through three, three CPU BLTBDAT/CON1 write
orders, and eight repeats. The line prime enables no DMA channel. The target is
a normal-mode, one-word B-to-D copy. In these kind48 records, the FF7800
observer stores the first and second D words at byte offsets 14 and 18. The
generic decoder names those fields `launch_ref` and `end_ref`; they are D-word
readbacks here, not timing references. The eight adjacent DMACONR reads occupy
`counter`, `icr`, `irq_beam`, `irq_level`, the two words combined by the decoder
as `saved_pc_offset`, `status`, and `aux`.

The A500 results support these measured rules for the held B word after the
line-mode prime:

- With one primed pixel and the target BDAT write skipped, the retained value
  is `shift_combine(0, raw_BDAT, BSHIFT, false)`.
- With two or three primed pixels and the target write skipped, it is
  `ROR(raw_BDAT, (BSHIFT - (pixels - 1)) & 15)`.
- Writing zero to BLTBDAT before setting target CON1 to normal mode produces
  `shift_combine(raw_BDAT, 0, (BSHIFT - pixels) & 15, false)`.
- Setting target CON1 to normal mode and then writing zero produces zero.

The first-pixel case is the exception to a simple circular-rotation rule. For
example, raw `$0001` with BSHIFT 1 and one prime pixel gives zero; raw `$a55a`
with BSHIFT 7 gives `$014a`; and raw `$1248` with BSHIFT 15 gives zero. With
two or three prime pixels, the circular rotation rule accounts for every
tested raw pattern and shift.

## DMA-history cross-check

A separate 168-row normal-B hold control uses seven primes, three target write
orders, and eight repeats. Its shared source ID is
`dc7c414cfc945bb7151027e08395b690b7836b8a1950cd77693454c57b2720aa`; the A500
packet SHA is `f495cb622dd747bcca3e01a1fe6e4aaf9f10802e7c76f7fe64205a0da8beb6fd`.
WinUAE matches A500's first-D word on all rows. Candidate 1 differs in 16
first-D words: after the pre-CON1 zero write, phase 5 gives A500/WinUAE `$5ffc`
versus candidate zero, and phase 6 gives `$a000` versus candidate zero, each
with eight repeats. Candidate 1 matches the corresponding skipped-write values
`$5fff` and `$a000`; the old Copperline baseline has 48 D-word mismatches in
this control. This points to B-channel DMA history contributing carry into a
later CPU BLTBDAT write.

This is the first candidate comparison, not a final emulator scorecard. The
maintainer will append the final-candidate counts after the next validation.

## C-DMA generalization capture

The follow-up `--line-hold-c-dma-matrix` capture enabled C DMA during the
line-mode prime while keeping A/B/D DMA disabled. It used the zero-filled C
buffer at `$14000`. The first line-prime D write was directed to `$15000`; after
the first pixel, line D writes followed C's pointer into `$14050` and `$140a0`.
The 256-byte zero buffer covers those later writes. The later normal-mode
target resets D to `$10000` before its one-word copy.

The A500 capture has source ID
`1b1847727efe84edb602a69fc9f78d6e01f9caa7fa38321549875b83173aa327` and
packet SHA-256
`4decda1716cd77cf4c2943f3e80fe2f758472bbc4a7186a52fb78099a9e2b73a`. It
contains 1,152 rows, with no skipped rows or timeouts. Matching each row to the
all-DMA-off matrix by pattern, low variant bits, phase, delay, and repeat gives
zero first-D-word differences. The second D word is zero in every row. Launch,
end, and bracketing beam fields are also identical across the two captures.
This supports the measured held-B rule under C DMA for this A500 and tested
matrix; it does not establish behavior for other Agnus revisions.

## Direct Copper BBUSY pilot handoff

The kind50 pilot has 528 rows: D-only widths 1/8/16, Copper/no-blit lists,
BLTPRI off/on, single/eight adjacent DMACONR reads, eleven phase gates and
two repeats. The sampling instructions execute from fixed ROM at FFC200.
Actual pre/post beam brackets are recorded; gate requests are not timestamps.
No-blit controls and single reads are necessary because CPU reads can perturb
bus arbitration. Toni Wilen's EAB Copper-start observations motivated these
controls; no timing change has yet been inferred from this new capture.
